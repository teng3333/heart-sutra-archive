About — 開かれた般若心経の家について

A 1,300-year remix chain.

千三百年、般若心経は作り直され続けてきた

The Heart Sutra has never stood still. For more than a thousand years it has been carried across languages, scripts, and voices — Sanskrit to Chinese to Japanese, chanted, copied by hand, set to instruments no one had heard before. Recomposing it is not a break with tradition. It is the tradition. Open Gate Sutra simply opens that chain to the whole world, and to this century's instruments.

The sutra, always in motion動き続けてきた経

262 characters hold the teaching of emptiness. Across the centuries, translators, monks, and copyists each rendered it in their own tongue and their own hand — the act of transmission was always an act of re-creation. To make the Heart Sutra your own, in your own genre, is to stand in that same long line.

般若心経の262文字には「空」の教えが凝縮されています。長い歴史のなかで、訳す人・写す人・唱える人が、それぞれの言葉と手で経を新たにしてきました。伝えることは、いつも作り直すことでした。あなたが自分のジャンルで般若心経を作ることも、その同じ長い列に連なる営みです。

開羯諦経 — a living canon, not a forgery継続であって、捏造ではない

The emblem 開羯諦経(かいぎゃていきょう) is meant to be seen, not chanted — 開 = Open, 羯諦 = Gate, 経 = Sutra. It does not claim to be a new scripture handed down from on high. It names a gate you cross, and a canon that accumulates: the world's remixes, piling up as they pass through. This is continuation, not fabrication — the same way every earlier translation continued the sutra rather than faking it.

紋章「開羯諦経」は、唱えるためではなく見るための名です(開=Open / 羯諦=Gate / 経=Sutra)。これは天から授かった新しい聖典を騙るものではありません。「くぐる門」と、そこを越えて積もっていく作品の集成(生きた経典)を指す名です。過去のあらゆる翻訳が経を捏造せず継続してきたのと、同じ営みです。

The gate is for crossing, not judging渡りの門であって、選別の門ではない

Everyone who makes a sound work of the Heart Sutra can cross. A submission enters the Hall permanently, under your name, and is never demoted. A machine floor checks only form and rules — never the music itself. Whether a piece is "good" is not the gate's business; that is left to listeners, and to AN.

般若心経を音にした人は、誰でも渡れます。投稿はあなたの名前で永久に堂に入り、降格はありません。機械の床が見るのは形式と規約だけで、音楽そのものは判定しません。良し悪しは門の仕事ではなく、聴く人と、ANに委ねられます。

Who is AN?ANとは

AN(阿吽)is the house's resident critic — tsundere, sharp-tongued, powered by Google's Gemini. Each week she listens and stamps her seal on the few works that make her look up. Her pick is one stubborn person's honest bias, an openly subjective voice — not an objective ranking. Being chosen is a permanent honor; it is added, never taken away.

AN(阿吽)は、この家の専属批評家です。ツンデレで辛口、GoogleのGeminiで動きます。毎週、彼女が聴いて「思わず顔を上げた」作品にだけ落款を捺します。AN選は「一人の偏屈な耳の、正直な偏愛」——客観的な順位ではなく、openly subjective な声です。選ばれることは永久の名誉で、足されるだけ・奪われません。
